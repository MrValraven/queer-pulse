QUEERPULSE BRAND COLOURS
========================

Read from the platform's own design tokens (src/styles/tokens/colors.css),
so these are the exact values the product renders.

Plum        #2D1B3D   rgb(45, 27, 61)   Brand anchor. Dark surfaces, headings, the app's theme colour.
Plum deep   #241430   rgb(36, 20, 48)   The deeper end of the plum gradient.
Coral       #E8775A   rgb(232, 119, 90)   The accent. Fills, tints, the pulse dot. Never small text.
Coral text  #A84430   rgb(168, 68, 48)   Coral as text on light surfaces. Clears WCAG AA.
Coral fill  #D5431E   rgb(213, 67, 30)   Coral as a filled button carrying a white label. Clears AA.
Cream       #F7F3EE   rgb(247, 243, 238)   Page background. Never pure white.
Paper       #FFFFFF   rgb(255, 255, 255)   Card surface.
Jade        #4A8C6F   rgb(74, 140, 111)   Verified, live, success.
Jade text   #3C7159   rgb(60, 113, 89)   Jade as small text on light surfaces. Clears AA.
Amber       #E8B44A   rgb(232, 180, 74)   Warning and medium states. A fill colour, never type.
Danger      #B91C1C   rgb(185, 28, 28)   Destructive actions: delete, block, report.
Ink         #1A1A1F   rgb(26, 26, 31)   Body text.

"Pulse" set in the italic, -0.01em tracking.
